# Demo vault

A small second brain in the layout the Command Center reads. Projects live in `1-Projects/<Name>/<Name>.md`, areas in `2-Areas/<Name>/<Name>.md`, tasks in `Tasks/T-### - <title>.md`, decisions as `D-### - <title>.md` in the owner's folder, sessions in `Sessions/`. Status lives in frontmatter. Bump `updated` on every change.
